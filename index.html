Universal Currency Converter

See every price on the web in your own currency — instantly.

What it does

  • Automatically detects prices on any website
  • Converts them to your home currency using live exchange rates
  • Shows the result inline next to the price, or in a hover tooltip
  • Works worldwide, with a full list of currencies to choose from

Private by design

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads
  • The pages you visit are read only in your browser and never sent anywhere
Privacy Policy
Universal Currency Converter is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google or any currency-data provider.
