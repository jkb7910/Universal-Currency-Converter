Privacy Policy

Universal Currency Converter · Last updated: June 7, 2026

Short version: This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your settings stay on your device, the pages you visit are processed only in your browser, and nothing you do is tracked or shared.

What the extension does

Universal Currency Converter detects prices on the web pages you view and displays the equivalent amount in a currency you choose. All detection and conversion happens locally, inside your own browser.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal identifier.
  • We do not collect, log, or transmit the content of the web pages you visit.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, fingerprinting, or advertising.
  • We do not create accounts or require sign-in.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties, because we do not have any.

Your settings

Your preferences — chosen currency, display style, on/off state, and the "$" mapping — are saved using Chrome's built-in storage. If you have Chrome sync enabled, Chrome may sync these settings across your own devices through your Google account. The developer of this extension has no access to that data and never receives it.

Reading page content

To find prices, the extension reads the text of pages you open. This reading happens entirely within your browser and in real time. Page text is never sent to the developer or to any server. It is used only to display a converted amount and is not stored.

Exchange-rate data

To show conversions, the extension requests current exchange rates from public providers (open.er-api.com and, as a fallback, frankfurter.app). These requests ask only for general exchange-rate tables and contain no personal information and nothing about the pages you visit. Rates are cached in your browser and refreshed periodically. Those providers may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) as described in their own privacy policies; the extension sends them nothing else.

Permissions, in plain language

  • Storage — to remember your currency and display preferences.
  • Access to the pages you visit — to read price text and show conversions on the sites you choose to browse.
  • Access to the rate providers above — to fetch live exchange rates.

Children

The extension is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
